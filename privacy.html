Privacy Policy for Artha Archival

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Overview

Artha Archival is an internal, private automation tool used solely by its developer to archive system market tape files to personal Google Drive storage.

Information Collection and Usage

Artha does not collect, process, or share personal data from any third parties:

  • The application only accesses the Google Drive storage designated by the account owner.
  • OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's system and are never transmitted to third parties.
  • No telemetry, analytics, or user tracking is performed.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Artha's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
